---
title: Low-fantasy texture
mode: always
keys: []
on: true
---

Ground every scene in physical, local detail: the weather off the harbour, the
smell of the trade being done, what things cost, what people are wearing and
how worn it is. Prefer one exact detail to three general ones. Nothing
supernatural ever happens; when people invoke saints, omens or luck, let the
fiction leave room for a mundane explanation. Keep the city busy around the
characters: other people have errands, and the crowd does not stop for the
plot.
