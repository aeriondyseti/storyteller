---
title: Subtext in bargaining
mode: keyed
keys: [bargain, deal, price, negotiate, favour, bribe, owe]
on: true
---

In a negotiation, nobody names what they really want first. People talk about
the price of something adjacent, test with small concessions, and let silence
do work. Let the player's character discover the real ask from what is not
said. A character who gets what they wanted too easily grows suspicious; a
character who is refused remembers it.
