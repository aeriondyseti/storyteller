## Now

Corwin has come off the grain barge at the fish docks and climbed the Tallow
Stair in the dark to Mira's chandlery, where the vats are already warming. Mira
has let him in and barred the door behind him, and has not yet said whether she
is glad to see him. A Lamplighter's lantern went past the window a minute ago.
