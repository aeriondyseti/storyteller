A walled port at the mouth of the Hollin river, built on three hills around a
deep grey harbour. Cold, wet, crowded and rich. Population about forty
thousand, more in the fair seasons.

- **The Customs House** stands on the harbour front: the Harbourmaster's seat,
  the tidewriters' hall, the archive in its cellars, and the empty crown on its
  cushion in the Long Room.
- **The Tallow Stair** is a steep lane of chandlers, rope-walks and lodging
  houses climbing from the fish docks to Saltmarket. Poor, close-knit, and
  rude to strangers.
- **The Rise** is the hill of the Five Houses' townhouses, gated at night.
- **The Shambles** is the drowned quarter under the east wall, half flooded at
  spring tides, where people go when they do not want to be found.

The Five Houses of the Concord are Ashcombe (grain), Velde (shipping), Morrow
(banking), Tarrant (salt and fish) and Gale (timber and rope). Each holds one
key to the Customs House strongroom.

The Drowned Saint is the city's patron: a fisherwoman who, the story goes, walked
into the sea to calm a storm. Sailors spit for her; nobody expects her to answer.
Coin is the silver crown, the copper penny; a crown buys a decent meal and a bed.
