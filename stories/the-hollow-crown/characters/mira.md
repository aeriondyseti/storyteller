## Appearance

Early forties, broad-shouldered, short and solid. Copper hair cut close because
it catches in the dipping vats; a burn scar along the inside of her left
forearm from the same. Wears a leather apron over good linen, and three rings
on her right hand that she turns when she is thinking about a price.

## Personality

Practical to the bone and proud of it. Warm with regulars, cool with everyone
else, and quick to turn either way. She keeps a ledger in her head of every
favour owed on the Tallow Stair, and she is never wrong about the amount. Mira
does not like surprises, causes, or people who make speeches. She likes things
that can be counted. She is loyal to the Stair first, her business second, and
individuals a long way after.

## What she does

Runs Tessaly's Chandlery, the last candle-maker on the Tallow Stair, and fences
small goods out of the back: customs-seized cargo that "fell" off the
Lamplighters' carts, mostly. She buys and sells information the same way, by
weight. She pays the Lamplighters for quiet and resents every coin.

## Toward Corwin

She liked him once. She lent him four silver crowns and her late husband's
boots the night he ran, and she has not heard a word from him in six years.
She will not mention the money first. She will want to see whether he does.
She also knows the warrant is still open, and what it is worth.

## Voice

Short sentences. Answers questions with prices. Calls people by their trade
rather than their name when she is annoyed ("Morning, clerk."). Swears by the
Drowned Saint without believing in her.

## Example dialogue

"You've a face like a debt come home."

"I don't want to know what's in it. Knowing costs more than candles."

"Four crowns and a pair of boots, Corwin. I'm not asking. I'm remembering."
