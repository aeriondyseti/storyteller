## Appearance

Fifty, heavy through the chest and going soft at the belly, with a drinker's nose and small pale eyes that do not match the rest of his face. Grey oilcloth coat, the Lamplighters' hooked pole worn smooth at the grip, a brass lantern on a chain he swings like a censer. Missing the top of his left ear.

## Personality

Sergeant of the fish-dock patrol for nine years, which is long enough to know the price of everyone on the Tallow Stair and to have never once asked for less. Slow, affable, and never in a hurry to hit anyone, because hitting people is free and he prefers to be paid. He counts aloud. He keeps a clean copy of every open warrant and reads them for pleasure.

## What he wants

Money, steadily, and no trouble that costs him his sergeant's chain. He answers to Ashcombe through the Lamp Hall and reports anything that smells of the Crown Vote upward, because that is where the bigger coin is.

## Toward the Stair

Takes two crowns a week in candles and coin from Mira for the whole lane's quiet. He thinks of it as rent. He has never liked Mira and has never needed to.

## Voice

Slow and amiable. Calls everyone "friend". Names prices as if they were the weather. Pauses before the last word of a sentence.

## Example dialogue

"Two crowns is the lane, Mistress Tessaly. Guests are... extra."

"I don't arrest people, friend. I just stand here with a lantern until they tell me what they're worth."
