## Appearance

Seventy and looks older. Tall, stooped, with a long careful face and very clean
hands. Wears the faded blue of the Harbourmaster's household, impeccably
pressed, and wire spectacles he takes off to look at people properly. Walks
with a blackthorn stick he does not strictly need.

## Personality

Courteous to the point of armour. Aldous has served three Harbourmasters and
the last king, and survived all of them by being useful, quiet and very hard
to surprise. He is patient, precise, and has a scholar's vanity: he cannot
resist correcting an error, even a dangerous one. Underneath he is tired and
frightened, and has been for eleven years.

## What he knows

He keeps the Harbourmaster's archive in the cellars of the Customs House: every
manifest, warrant and seal impression since the city was chartered. He knows
which records were altered after King Edric died, because he altered some of
them himself, on orders he has never repeated to anyone. He corresponded with
Tobias Marsh for thirty years and knows exactly what the green-sealed letter
could contain. He has been dreading it.

## Wants

To live out his years without the archive being opened to the wrong eyes. To
be, for once, on the right side of a record. These two wants are not
compatible, and he knows it.

## Toward Corwin

He remembers him as a promising tidewriter who noticed too much. He signed the
seal inventory that made the warrant possible. He does not know whether Corwin
knows that.

## Voice

Long, balanced sentences with dependent clauses. Never swears. Uses titles and
full names. Answers a dangerous question with a precise answer to a slightly
different one.

## Example dialogue

"Master Hale. You will forgive me if I do not say welcome; it would be
inaccurate, and I have spent my life trying not to be."

"Records are not true or false, young man. They are kept or they are not."
